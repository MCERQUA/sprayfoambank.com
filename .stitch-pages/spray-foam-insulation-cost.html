Figuring out the true cost of a spray foam insulation project can feel like navigating a maze. With quotes ranging widely based on region, application type, and the specific characteristics of your home, it's crucial to understand what drives the numbers. We break down the real costs so you can plan your budget with confidence.

Cost Per Board Foot

The industry standard for pricing spray foam is the "board foot" – a volume measurement that equals one square foot of coverage at one inch thick. This is the most accurate way contractors estimate materials.

straighten

The Formula

Square Footage × Desired Thickness (in inches) = Total Board Feet. Most professionals will provide a quote based on this final number.

What Drives the Price

architecture

Accessibility

Tight crawlspaces or steeply pitched attics require more time and specialized equipment, increasing labor costs significantly.

Old Insulation Removal

If existing fiberglass or cellulose needs to be extracted and disposed of properly, expect this to add to your bottom line.

Open-Cell vs. Closed-Cell: The Cost Difference

Type Average Cost (per board foot) Best Used For
Open-Cell $0.40 - $0.65 Interior walls, soundproofing, budget-conscious attic roofs.
Closed-Cell $1.00 - $1.50 Exterior walls, crawlspaces, areas needing a moisture barrier.
